# Community conduct

This policy applies to DIDMail's discussions, pull requests and other community
interactions associated with this repository.

## Expectations

Treat participants respectfully regardless of experience, background or identity.
Ask for clarification, give actionable feedback, and distinguish criticism of
an idea from criticism of a person. Allow time for volunteer responses and make
room for people using different languages or accessibility tools.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, disclose personal or
confidential information without permission, or post spam and deceptive links.
Sexual harassment, repeated unwanted contact and targeted abuse are unacceptable.
Do not use the forum to solicit recovery phrases, private keys or payments
purporting to be required for support.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's report-content controls for abusive content. For a concern requiring
maintainer attention, contact the repository owner,
[@shenxiuqiang](https://github.com/shenxiuqiang), through an available private
contact route. If none is available, ask for a private reporting channel without
posting the incident's sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities use
the separate [security process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may request a correction, remove inappropriate content, close or lock
disruptive threads, or restrict participation when necessary. Responses should
be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected participants' privacy.
Repeated or severe violations may result in exclusion from the community.
